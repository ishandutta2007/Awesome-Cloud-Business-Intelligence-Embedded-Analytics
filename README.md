## 🏢 SaaS / Commercial Platforms



The cloud BI and embedded analytics market spans **per-seat platforms** (Power BI, Tableau) and **capacity-based or per-viewer models** (Looker, QuickSight, Sigma). Pricing models vary dramatically: **Power BI** charges **$14/user/month for Pro** and **$24/user/month for Premium Per User**, with capacity-based Fabric SKUs starting around **$262/month (F2)** for embedded analytics . **Looker** uses sales-quoted enterprise pricing — typical deployments land between **$36,000 and $66,000+ per year**, with per-user costs from **~$400/year for viewers to $1,665 for developers** . **Amazon QuickSight** offers **$3/month per reader** or pay-per-session pricing, making it cost-effective for large read-only audiences . **Metabase Cloud** starts at **$100/month** (first five users) with Pro at **$575/month** adding embedding and SSO . **Sigma Computing** charges **$25/user/month**, while **Mode** is **$35/user/month** .

## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects



*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟



- **[Apache Superset](https://github.com/apache/superset)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/apache/superset?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/apache/superset/stargazers)  

  **The most complete open-source BI platform**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **40+ chart types, full SQL editor, broadest warehouse coverage** — native connectors for Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, Redshift, Postgres, MySQL, Trino, ClickHouse, and **40+ more via SQLAlchemy** . **Semantic layer** built around datasets and reusable metrics compiled to SQL that runs in the warehouse. **dbt manifest integration** surfaces models and exposures in the UI. **Embedded SDK included free** — server issues guest token with RLS rules, frontend loads via iframe SDK . **Stateless web tier scales horizontally** for customer-facing concurrency — used in production by Airbnb, Dropbox, and Netflix . **Deployment**: Docker Compose (~10 min), Helm charts available. **The default open-source answer for Tableau-class dashboarding** . 🚀



- **[Metabase](https://github.com/metabase/metabase)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/metabase/metabase?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/metabase/metabase/stargazers)  

  **Most popular open-source BI by GitHub stars**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **Single JAR file deployment** — `java -jar metabase.jar` and you have a BI tool . **Visual query builder** translates clicks into SQL for non-technical users — the only open-source tool where a marketing manager can build dashboards without SQL training . **Written in Clojure**, runs anywhere Docker runs. **OSS static embedding free** with "Powered by Metabase" badge; **Pro Interactive Embedding ($575/month + $12/user)** unlocks white-label, row-level security, and SSO . **Limitations**: visual query builder hits ceiling on complex joins; lighter semantic layer than Superset; embedding paywalled for white-label . **Best for**: teams where the people asking questions are not the people who write SQL . 🎯



- **[Grafana](https://github.com/grafana/grafana)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/grafana/grafana?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/grafana/grafana/stargazers)  

  **Operational monitoring and observability leader**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **Default dashboard for Prometheus, InfluxDB, Elasticsearch, and dozens of time-series backends**. **Massive plugin ecosystem**, production-grade alerting, and large community. **Strong for real-time KPI monitoring** — API response time, uptime, queue depth, throughput, sensor/IoT metrics . **Trade-off**: assumes you know what you want to see — no exploratory drag-and-drop experience. Better for technical operations than business storytelling . **Grafana Cloud free tier** generous; paid from **$29/month** . **Best for**: DevOps, SRE, and infrastructure teams tracking live metrics . 📈



- **[Redash](https://github.com/getredash/redash)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/getredash/redash?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/getredash/redash/stargazers)  

  **SQL-first query and dashboard tool**, BSD-2-Clause licensed. **Does one thing**: write SQL, render charts, pin to dashboards — minimal overhead . **Supports 35+ data sources** including all major warehouses . **No visual query builder, no semantic layer**. **⚠️ Maintenance concern**: **Databricks acquired Redash in 2020** and shut down hosted service; **development slowed dramatically** — only three releases between January 2025 and March 2026 after four years without a release . **Community forum read-only**. **Limitations**: every dashboard requires SQL knowledge; no native embedded analytics; no multi-tenant customer analytics; no built-in NLQ . **Best for**: SQL-fluent small engineering teams where "write query, see chart, share link" is the entire workflow — but **choosing Redash in 2026 is a bet on a project with unclear long-term maintenance** . ⚠️



- **[Lightdash](https://github.com/lightdash/lightdash)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/lightdash/lightdash?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/lightdash/lightdash/stargazers)  

  **dbt-native BI platform**, open-source. **Reads your dbt project directly** — models, metrics, dimensions, descriptions become an explorable BI layer. **No re-defining metrics in a separate tool** — if dbt says `revenue = sum(amount) where status = 'paid'`, Lightdash uses that definition everywhere . **First-class dbt awareness** — the only open-source BI built from the ground up for dbt teams . **Limitations**: **not a standalone BI tool** — requires dbt project; chart builder lacks depth of Superset for complex visualizations . **Lightdash Cloud** starts free for small teams. **Best for**: analytics engineering teams running dbt who are tired of metric definitions drifting between dbt and their BI tool . 🔧



- **[Evidence](https://github.com/evidence-dev/evidence)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/evidence-dev/evidence?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/evidence-dev/evidence/stargazers)  

  **Code-first analytics with Markdown**, MIT licensed. **Build dashboards by writing Markdown with embedded SQL** — Git-versioned, static-deployable . **No drag-and-drop for broad business users** — for creating tailored, developer-controlled analytical experiences . **$0 hosting** on Vercel/Netlify for static deploy. **Evidence Cloud**: Team **$15/user/mo**, Pro **$25/user/mo**; Embedded is **Enterprise-tier only** . **Best for**: code-first analytics and embedded storytelling where developer control matters more than broad self-service . 📝



- **[Cube](https://github.com/cube-js/cube)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/cube-js/cube?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/cube-js/cube/stargazers)  

  **Semantic layer and API layer**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Not a chart library** — provides semantic layer, caching, and API layer that sits between your data and any visualization tool . **Cube Cloud**: Free → Starter **$40/dev/mo** → Premium **$80/dev/mo** (Premium includes embedded dashboards) . **2026 positioning**: Cube Copilot, Tesseract modeling engine, Visual Modeler, Data Access Policies. Joined **Open Semantic Interchange spec** (January 2026) . **The strongest non-BI building block for productizing a dashboard offering** — pair with Next.js, Tremor, Recharts, and Postgres RLS for a productized SaaS analytics stack . 🧊



- **[Graphic Walker](https://github.com/Kanaries/graphic-walker)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Kanaries/graphic-walker?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Kanaries/graphic-walker/stargazers)  

  **Embeddable visualization analysis React component**, open-source. **Not another heavy BI platform** — composable, embeddable analysis components that integrate seamlessly with React applications . **Pass data as props** — render charts via drag-and-drop in any app. **DuckDB engine** for fast local/edge computation; **pushdown to ClickHouse, StarRocks, or Snowflake** for enterprise scale . **Full control over rendering and styling** — use your own chart library, merge multiple libraries, build domain-specific visualizations. **Pricing**: Startup **$199/month/domain** (or **$1,900/year**) . **Best for**: embedding analytics into SaaS products, internal analysis consoles, and data exploration workbenches . 🎨



- **[Kibana](https://github.com/elastic/kibana)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/elastic/kibana?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/elastic/kibana/stargazers)  

  **Elastic Stack visualization**, Elastic License 2.0 / SSPL. **Part of Elastic Stack** — focused on **log and event data analysis and visualization** . **Primarily used only with Elasticsearch** — not general-purpose for business KPI dashboarding across business functions . **Limited native alerting capabilities** compared to Grafana; customization focused on log/event data . **Best for**: organizations already invested in Elasticsearch for logs, events, security analysis, and operational search-driven dashboards . 🔍



- **[Looker Studio](https://lookerstudio.google.com/)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/...?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/.../stargazers)  

  **Free Google BI product**, proprietary (Google). **Free for base product**; **Looker Studio Pro $9/user/project/month annual** . **Embed is iframe-only** with "powered by Looker Studio" feel . **Best for**: marketing and Google Ads or GA4 reporting where the firm is fine with the branding . **Slows down badly on large joins, weak governance** . 📊

## ⚠️ Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️

- **Open source is not free** — Metabase and Superset are excellent with zero license cost, but their real cost is **a person**. Someone runs upgrades, wires SSO, tunes cache, debugs the query that took the database down at month end, and answers questions when a chart breaks. If your BI team is one analyst with a full workload, a paid tool with support is cheaper in practice .

- **Redash is in maintenance-only status** — Databricks acquired it in 2020, hosted service shut down, and development slowed to roughly one release per year. The GitHub release history shows **four years without a release** after v10.1.0 (November 2021) before three community-maintained versions shipped between January 2025 and March 2026. **Choosing Redash in 2026 is a bet on a project with unclear long-term maintenance** .

- **Metabase embedding is paywalled** — OSS static embedding is free with "Powered by Metabase" badge; **Pro Interactive Embedding ($575/month + $12/user) unlocks white-label and RLS** .

- **Looker embedded deployments reach $200K–$300K+ annually** — base platform ~$60K/yr, viewers ~$400/year each, developers ~$1,665/year. **Out of scope for small teams** .

- Open-source solutions (Superset, Metabase, Lightdash, Evidence) provide self-hosted ownership and transparency, but enterprise-grade SLA guarantees, managed HA, and 24/7 support remain primarily commercial offerings. **For production-grade with HA, SSO, RLS, and audit, self-hosted takes days-to-weeks; managed collapses that to hours** . 📊
